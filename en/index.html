01 — About me

I love figuring out how a business works — and making it work better.

I'm from Barquisimeto, in Venezuela's Lara State. I trained at IUJO, Instituto Universitario Jesús Obrero, earning an associate degree in Computer Science — that's where I learned to turn ideas into solutions that actually work. Today I'm also studying Telematics Engineering at UCLA.

I started out building websites for businesses, then worked across banking, fintech and healthcare, turning what each client needed into clear solutions. In 2025 I co-founded Tripealo. I believe in Venezuela, and in building from here.

FAQ

In a nutshell

Who is Alejandro Hernández?

I'm from Barquisimeto, in Venezuela's Lara State. I work where finance, strategy and product meet, and today I'm co-founder and CFO of Tripealo, the startup that puts everything you need to travel in Venezuela into one app.

Where did Alejandro Hernández study?

I earned an associate degree in Computer Science at IUJO, Instituto Universitario Jesús Obrero (2022–2025), and I study Telematics Engineering at UCLA, Universidad Centroccidental Lisandro Alvarado (2022–present).

What is Tripealo?

A travel experiences marketplace that puts the whole country in the traveler's hand: Venezuela's transfers, routes and tours in one place. Its motto: more destinations, fewer limits.

What to do in Barquisimeto and Lara?

In Barquisimeto: catch one of its legendary sunsets, visit the Cathedral and the Obelisk, stroll Parque del Este and, in January, join the Divina Pastora procession. Around Lara: Cubiro and Sanare for mountain air, Carora for its vineyards, Quíbor for crafts and El Tocuyo for colonial history.
